Privacy Policy

Tab Manipulator is designed to perform its work locally in your browser.

In short: Tab Manipulator does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, browsing history, or usage data.

Data handled by the extension

To provide tab rotation and scheduled refresh, Tab Manipulator may process tab URLs, titles, pinned state, position, extension settings, and active schedule state. This information is used only to perform the actions you request.

Storage and transmission

  • Settings and active schedule information are stored in extension-local browser storage.
  • No browsing metadata, settings, error details, or usage data is sent to BWork0 or any third party.
  • The extension includes no analytics, advertising, remote logging, or remote executable code.
  • No account is required.

When the extension refreshes a tab, the browser may contact that page and its service providers in the same way it would during a manual refresh. The privacy practices of those websites are governed by their own policies.

Browser permissions

  • tabs — read eligible tab details, activate selected tabs, and reload scheduled tabs.
  • storage — save your settings and active schedule state locally.
  • alarms — schedule reliable rotation and refresh work.

Tab Manipulator does not request browsing-history, identity, content-script, scripting, or broad website-access permissions.

Your choices

You can stop active schedules at any time. Stopping a schedule removes its runtime target information. Removing the extension deletes its remaining extension-local data through your browser.

Children’s privacy

Tab Manipulator does not knowingly collect information from children or from any other user because it does not collect personal information.

Changes to this policy

If the extension’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released. The effective date at the top identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit the support page or open an issue in the Tab Manipulator GitHub repository. Do not include private URLs or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Tab Manipulator is published by BWork0.
