Before reporting a problem

  • Confirm that the selected tab is a normal website. Browsers prevent extensions from manipulating internal pages such as settings and extension stores.
  • For dependable background timing, use an interval of 30 seconds or longer. Ten-second rotation is best effort and may be delayed when the browser suspends background work.
  • If a recovered schedule says it needs attention, select the intended tabs again. The extension deliberately avoids acting when it cannot identify a stored target safely.
  • Try stopping the schedule and starting it again after checking the selected tabs.

What to include

Include your browser name and version, the extension version, what you expected, what happened, and clear steps to reproduce the issue.

Public GitHub issues can be read by anyone. Remove private URLs, tab titles, account details, and other sensitive information before posting.

Supported browsers

The first release supports Chrome 120 or newer, Microsoft Edge 120 or newer, and Firefox 140 or newer. Other Chromium-based browsers may work but are not primary release targets.
